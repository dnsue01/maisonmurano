# Storefront (home, shop, product, cart)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: IG/TikTok link-in-bio buyer on a phone; action: add a limited piece to the cart before it sells out. Proof: their real product cutouts, on-model photos, reviews. Constraint: structure follows Daniel's TIER CLUB (marquee, sticky centered header, hero, featured, lookbook, brand break, about, newsletter, footer, product page, cart drawer); checkout demo-only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The drop is a tattoo flash sheet: every garment is a numbered design you pick off the parlour wall. Refuses the generic full-bleed-banner-plus-product-grid streetwear template.
OWN-WORLD: Parlour-wall black ground; flash paper panels in bone with thick black linework borders; traditional flash palette — flash red, bottle green, mustard — as stamps, numbers and price tags, never gradients. Blackletter display (tattoo lettering), condensed grotesk labels, stamped numerals #01. Corners square, 2px ink rules, pinned-sheet tilt only on sheets.
STORY: Visitor sees the brand's campaign and its current drop as a flash sheet, understands pieces are few and numbered, picks one, adds it in two taps.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Campaign photo band with MAISON MURANO in blackletter set in a banner scroll; immediately below, overlapping, the flash sheet "DROP — PICK YOUR PIECE" with available pieces as numbered cutout designs with price tags and quick add.
FORM: Tattoo flash sheet, candidate 3 of 7 (tour merch, the room, tattoo flash, zine, Murano glass, army surplus, VHS sleeves). seed:ce09f53e22b06646
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
